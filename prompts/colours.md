## Unique fabric colours (7)

- Burgundy #4C181F — deep wine red
- Navy #1E1D28 — very dark navy, almost black-blue
- Grey #6D6C76 — cool slate grey, RGB 109,108,118 (locked anchor)
- Black #171718 — near-neutral black sherpa
- Olive #4F4E44 — dark green-grey olive, RGB 79,78,68
- Off-White (cream) #EEEAE4 — warm cream white
- White (snow) #E5E2DE — slightly cooler white

## Notes

- Grey anchor: the solid Grey and the Grey/Black Mixed share the SAME grey (Smael's word, 25 Nov) = #6D6C76. The grey-black product front photo reads lighter/lavender (#9D94A5) only because that photo is over-lit — do NOT use it as the grey.
- Cream vs white: measured a few points apart (#EEEAE4 vs #E5E2DE); near-identical warm whites, difference is partly the two photos' lighting. They are different fabrics by design (Off-White vs Snow-White).
- Black: the grey-black sides read #2D262D (warm, light-struck) vs #171718 on the All Black front and the black texture close-up — #171718 is the truer black.
