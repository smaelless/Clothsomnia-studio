Sister look to RYUK (`references/ryuk/`). Same idea, pink and glam instead of black and stealth.

## The look (3 pieces, always together)

### 1. Balaclava
- Pink ribbed-knit balaclava covering the whole head and face: no eye or mouth opening, a closed knit face
- Fine vertical rib running top to bottom, with a seam line up the centre of the crown
- Ribbed collar at the neck that flares slightly outward at the bottom edge
- Slightly heathered/marbled yarn (darker magenta flecks through the pink)
- Colour, measured from the sheet: lit areas about `#D5688C` (range `#CE6083`-`#D26185`), shadow folds about `#A63F5E`

### 2. Sunglasses
- Cat-eye shape: wide frame with upswept outer corners, a small bridge, sitting on the knit face at eye level
- Frame: translucent pink with a fine pink glitter finish
- Crystal/rhinestone embellishments clustered on the upper outer corners of the frame (both sides), in clear and pale pink stones
- Lenses: gradient, dark rose-brown at the top fading lighter and pinker toward the bottom (about `#783038` to `#C56674`)
- Temple arms: translucent rose-gold/pale pink with a glitter texture, visible in the three-quarter view
- Level and symmetrical in the front view, same rule as RYUK

### 3. Gloves
- Pink ribbed-knit gloves, matching the balaclava
- Ribbed cuff, vertical rib continuing up the back of the hand, knit fingers
- Colour: back of hand about `#D5658A`, palm about `#E77499`
