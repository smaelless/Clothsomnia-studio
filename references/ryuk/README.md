## The look (3 pieces, always together)

### 1. Balaclava (lighter version)
- Charcoal / dark slate ribbed-knit balaclava covering the whole head and face: no eye or mouth opening, a closed knit face
- Colour, measured from the sheet: lit areas about `#282C33` (range `#272C35`-`#292D34`), with a cool blue-grey cast; deep folds under the cheek and jaw about `#161820`. Not pure black: the rib and texture stay readable
- Fine vertical rib running top to bottom, with a seam line up the centre of the crown
- Slightly heathered yarn with faint green and purple flecks in the shadows
- Ribbed collar at the neck that flares slightly at the bottom edge

### 2. Sunglasses
- Black rectangular (wayfarer-style) sunglasses, glossy black frame, dark smoked-grey lenses
- Sit on the knit face at eye level, straight and level, symmetrical in the front view
- Thick black temple arms, visible in the three-quarter view

### 3. Gloves
- Matching charcoal ribbed-knit gloves with ribbed cuffs, vertical rib continuing up the back of the hand
- Colour: back of hand about `#292D34`, palm about `#383E45` (slightly lighter in the light)
